Spam
====

Logs comments already marked spam by WordPress. Premium also logs Akismet discards. |WPf2b| does not classify spam; it records the classification for fail2ban (hard).

The :ref:`quickstart_spam_protection` card enables spam logging. The individual control is on the Logging tab in Advanced settings.
