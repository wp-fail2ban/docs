Email-only login
================

When enabled, a login that uses the username (not the email address) is blocked and logged. Combined with user-enumeration blocking this is the Username Protection card: attackers cannot confirm a login name and then stuff passwords against it.

Users must sign in with the account email. Tell them before you enable it.

In 6.3 this is on the Block tab.
